# Execution integration and capture assessment review

This slice connects the experiment ledger to the local supervisor, adds an exact native-preflight output contract, and turns archived CoworkeRL metrics into durable diagnostic requests. It advances execution control and evidence-driven decisions without enabling an unqualified trainer or treating process success as scientific success.

## Independent findings and corrections

**Cancellation needed its own authority and receipt.** The original worker exposed launch acceptance in a cancellation result. That could not acknowledge a later stop command. The worker now returns the stop-command status and exact stop observation; its required cancellation callback reaches the transport's final pre-effect check after the asynchronous ownership query. Revocation and thrown authority checks deny the stop. Offline tests exercise that race; the native suite exercises authorized cancellation.

**Restored databases must agree on the full declaration.** Independent bridge review constructed valid ledger and journal records with identical action/plan labels but different commands. The bridge now compares the durable operation intent's job and full declaration digest with the journal before recovery, observation or cancellation. A missing binding remains unknown. The regression rejects acceptance from the mismatched restored journal without calling start again.

**Status evidence must identify the query that produced it.** Reading `latestObservation` after awaiting a worker result could attach a later observation to an earlier result. Worker results now carry their own journal ID/sequence, and the bridge validates and cites that exact record. An independent regression injects a later terminal observation between worker completion and bridge continuation; the earlier running result still cites its own receipt.

**Preparation must have a stable wait bound.** A read-only deployment resolver that ignores cancellation must not block the per-action recovery queue forever or start work from a late return. The bridge bounds the wait, discards late results and captures its validated timeout at construction. Review tests mutate the caller's options after construction and confirm the original bound remains in effect. No resolver is permitted to hide launch/provisioning effects in this phase.

**Invalid step indices belong to the structural check.** The report reader omits invalid numeric indices while retaining a diagnostic. The first assessor checked the remaining steps but missed those rejected records in its ordering rule. Independent tests use real captures with negative, string and NaN indices; the corrected rule now fails structural integrity and requests sequence inspection. The changed rule-definition digest creates a new assessment identity, preserving the earlier archived version.

**Native preflight is narrower than full admission.** The source reconfirmation at CoworkeRL `84b219f64f1152f8b02babea388bfbbfff41e75a` shows that the success marker is printed before objective metadata generation can fail. The interpreter requires complete matching output and exit zero. Independent source review checked objective declarations, resolved actor pins, the scripted canary and actual training/checkpoint paths. Numerical qualification, serving, CUDA/model execution, resume and immutable input closure remain explicitly pending.
